# Harbor HL-7 Solar Lantern: Owner's Guide

This guide describes the Harbor HL-7, a fictional solar camping lantern. The text was written for the DocuMind eval suite and is released into the public domain (CC0). Any similarity to a real product is accidental.

## Overview

The Harbor HL-7 is a folding solar lantern for camping, boats and power cuts. It weighs 410 grams and folds down to a disc 3.5 centimetres thick. The body is made of recycled polycarbonate and the handle is stainless steel. It has a rated water resistance of IP67, so it can sit under one metre of fresh water for up to 30 minutes.

## Battery and Charging

The lantern holds a 5,200 mAh lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) battery. The built-in solar panel charges a flat battery in about 14 hours of direct sunlight. The USB-C port charges it in 3 hours from a 10 watt adapter. A green ring around the power button stays on while charging and turns off when the battery is full. The battery is rated for 2,000 full charge cycles before it drops to 80 percent of its original capacity.

## Light Modes

The HL-7 has four light modes. Press the power button once to cycle through them.
Low mode gives 50 lumens and runs for 72 hours.
Medium mode gives 150 lumens and runs for 24 hours.
High mode gives 400 lumens and runs for 8 hours.
SOS mode flashes the international distress signal in Morse code and runs for 120 hours.
Hold the power button for 2 seconds to turn the lantern off from any mode.

## Power Bank Feature

The lantern can charge a phone. Plug the phone into the USB-A port on the base. The USB-A port gives up to 5 volts at 1 amp. The lantern stops sharing power when its own battery falls to 20 percent, so you always keep some light.

## Care and Storage

Wipe the solar panel with a soft damp cloth once a month. Do not use solvents or alcohol on the panel. For long storage, charge the battery to about 60 percent and keep the lantern somewhere between 10 and 25 degrees Celsius. Top up the charge every three months while it is stored.

## Troubleshooting

If the lantern does not turn on, charge it with USB-C for 30 minutes and try again. If the light flickers in High mode, the battery is low; switch to Low mode or charge it. To do a factory reset, hold the power button and the mode button together for 10 seconds until the light blinks twice.

## Warranty and Support

The Harbor HL-7 comes with a 3-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty does not cover damage from salt water left to dry inside the USB ports. Rinse the ports with fresh water after use at sea. Support requests go to the Harbor support desk, which answers within two working days.
